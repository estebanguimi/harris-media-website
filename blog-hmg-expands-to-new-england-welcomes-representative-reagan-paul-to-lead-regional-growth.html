HMG EXPANDS TO NEW ENGLAND, WELCOMESREPRESENTATIVE REAGAN PAUL TO LEAD REGIONAL GROWTH

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Augusta, Maine – November 2025

Harris Media Group (HMG), a leading national digital communications and marketing firm, announced today that it is expanding its presence in New England and officially planting a flag in Maine. While the firm has had a presence in the state for the past two years, this marks the opening of its first dedicated office in Augusta.

As part of this expansion, HMG is proud to welcome Representative Reagan Paul to the team. A gifted communicator and grassroots leader, Paul is one of the rising voices in the Republican Party—not just in Maine and New England, but across the country. She brings a breadth of legislative policy knowledge and digital communications experience to HMG’s growing New England operations.

“We’re excited to continue investing in this region,” said HMG CEO Vincent Harris. “Republicans aren’t giving up on New England. We’ve seen tremendous opportunity here through our award-winning work for the New Hampshire State Senate Caucus, where our digital strategy has helped expand the caucus to a supermajority over the past three election cycles. We’ve also been proud to work with the Maine Republican Party and as part of the team that launched Governor Paul LePage’s run for Congress in Maine’s Second District this year, as well as lead statewide campaigns for leaders like Linda McMahon and Bob Stefanowski in Connecticut, and governor’s and congressional races throughout Maine and New Hampshire. Our firm has proudly worked in New Hampshire on multiple presidential campaigns, including for now-Ambassador Mike Huckabee, Governor Rick Perry, Speaker Newt Gingrich, Senator Rand Paul, and Miami Mayor Francis Suarez. Reagan’s leadership and communication skills will help us continue to build and innovate in this region.”

Representative Paul said, “I’m thrilled to join HMG and help bring forward-looking strategy, creative storytelling, and cutting-edge digital communication to campaigns and organizations across New England. It’s exciting to work alongside a team that’s redefining how conservatives connect, communicate, and win.”

About HMG: Harris Media Group (HMG) is a full-service digital marketing and communications agency specializing in political, corporate, and advocacy campaigns. The firm has run digital operations for Fortune 500 companies, twice for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s successful elections, and for U.S. Senators John Cornyn, Ted Cruz, Mitch McConnell, Rick Scott, and others.

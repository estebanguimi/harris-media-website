BIO

Raised in Florida, Joseph is an Account Coordinator at Harris Media LLC, where he helps manage digital campaigns, social media strategy, and content development. He graduated from the University of Florida with a Bachelor’s degree in Journalism and Communications and is currently working toward becoming a “double-Gator” while pursuing his Master’s degree in Public Relations at UF. He pairs this academic foundation with firsthand political communications experience, having previously interned in Washington, D.C., for Congressman Byron Donalds.

Drawn to Harris Media’s mission and values, Joseph brings a strategic, fast-paced approach to his work, combining a sharp understanding of media relations with a passion for compelling storytelling. He looks forward to driving client success by crafting high-impact digital campaigns that connect with audiences and leave a lasting impression.

Outside of work, he enjoys playing sports, hanging out with friends, and channeling his creativity into producing engaging social media content.

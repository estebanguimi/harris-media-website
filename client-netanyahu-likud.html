AI
THE BRIEF

TURN SUPPORTERS INTO CALLERS

Israeli elections are won on turnout, and turnout is won one conversation at a time. For Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s Likud National Liberal Party, HMG set out to scale those conversations with technology no campaign had used before.

The result was the first-ever AI-generated and AI-operated chatbot in presidential political history: a bot that met supporters on the messaging apps they already used and guided them to call fellow voters.

THE PLAYBOOK

WHAT WE DID

01

BUILD THE BOT

An AI-generated, AI-operated chatbot designed to hold real conversations with supporters — the first of its kind in presidential politics.

02

MEET VOTERS WHERE THEY ARE

Deployed on Telegram and Facebook, the platforms Likud supporters already used every day.

03

TURN CHATS INTO CALLS

The bot facilitated 8,323 voters making phone calls through Telegram and 8,676 voters through Facebook.

04

TRACK EVERY RESPONSE

Overall responses and call numbers were tracked, so the campaign could see participation by channel.

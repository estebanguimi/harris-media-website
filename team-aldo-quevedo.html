BIO

Aldo was born in Mexicali, Mexico, and currently lives in Brazil. He is a passionate and dedicated developer with a strong focus on building modern, scalable, and visually engaging websites and web applications. His expertise spans across the MERN stack, WordPress, and Webflow.

With a strong command of technologies such as PHP, React, JavaScript, and various database systems, both relational and non-relational, Aldo enjoys crafting efficient and elegant solutions that enhance user experience and performance.

Originally graduating with honors as a Mechanical Engineer from UABC in Mexico, Aldo discovered his true passion in programming and decided to pursue it as his lifelong career. His background in engineering gives him a unique analytical approach to problem-solving and system design.

Outside of his professional work, Aldo has a deep love for learning new languages and exploring different cultures. He also enjoys watching movies and series, playing League of Legends, and spending quality time with his family.

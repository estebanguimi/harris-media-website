GRAPHIC DESIGN INTERNSHIP (SPRING 2026)

APPLY NOW →ALL ROLES
MIAMI, FL · HYBRID

We are looking for a talented and driven design student to join our team for the Spring 2026 semester. You will be a part of our design staff aiding in the creation of graphics for social media, digital advertising, and splash pages. An ideal candidate would also have AfterEffects knowledge or knowledge of making animations/GIFs in Photoshop.

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Work with the Design Team to create engaging digital graphics
  • Using a detailed eye for design, you will create high-quality graphics and digital ads, while adhering to a strict set of brand guidelines
  • Attend weekly learnings to stay up-to-date on the latest design trends and to learn best practices
  • Learn from industry leading designers how to apply design theory to a variety of campaigns and advanced techniques using the Adobe Creative Suite
  • Join interdisciplinary meetings to observe and contribute fresh creative solutions
  • Create layouts and compositions for animation by motion designers
  • Participate in collaboration efforts for various projects and content

Requirements

  • Experience or interest in Republican/conservative political campaigns, organizations, etc.
  • Enthusiasm for the design process and attentiveness to different project goals
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong application of typography
  • Proficiency in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign
  • Knowledge of After Effects is a plus
  • Knowledge of Sketch and InVision is a plus

Internship Details

  • Timeframe: The Spring 2026 Internship starts on January 19th and ends on May 15th
  • Workload: 10-15 hours per week
  • Location: Remote
  • Compensation: Internship positions are unpaid, but can be completed for school credit

To apply, please send your resume to hr@harrismediallc.com with “Spring 2026 Graphic Design Internship” in the subject line. We look forward to reviewing your application!

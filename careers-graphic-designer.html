GRAPHIC DESIGNER

APPLY NOW →ALL ROLES
MIAMI, FL · HYBRID

Harris Media is seeking a talented, innovative, Graphic Designer with a great portfolio to join our growing team on a full-time basis. Specifically, we’re looking for someone with experience designing websites, digital ads, video editing, and social media graphics. An ideal candidate would also have After Effects knowledge or knowledge of making animations/GIFs in Photoshop. The ideal candidate would have experience in leading the direction of graphic design and branding. Our team works a hybrid schedule M-W in our Downtown Miami office and Th-F remote.

Requirements

  • Strong knowledge of the Adobe Creative Suite.
  • Ability to design websites.
  • Basic knowledge of capabilities of HTML and CSS as it pertains to designing for web.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with our account executive staff.
  • Solid understanding of typography and graphic design principles.
  • Ability to create animated GIFs
  • The ability to balance multiple projects at a time
  • Basic knowledge of UX pertaining to websites.

Nice to have

  • Strong knowledge of HTML and CSS
  • Experience designing for HTML5 ads using Google web designer
  • Knowledge of After Effects and how to create motion graphics
  • Knowledge of Filming video and knowledge of photography
  • Knowledge of Sketch, Adobe XD, and/or Figma
  • Webflow experience
  • Experience working for Republican or center-right campaigns or advocacy groups
  • Pay: $40,000 – $50,000 per year
  • Benefits: Health, dental, and vision insurance plans, paid time off, 401(k) matching, and opportunities for bonuses and commission pay

To apply, please submit your resume to hr@harrismediallc.com. Please include a link to your portfolio with your application.

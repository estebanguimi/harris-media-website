BIO

Paige Manning is an Account Coordinator at Harris Media LLC, where she contributes to digital campaigns, social media strategies, and content development for a diverse portfolio of clients. She looks forward to learning more about the political media landscape and continuing to grow her skills in digital strategy, communications, and campaign work.

She recently graduated from the University of Miami, earning a Bachelor of Business Administration degree with a major in marketing and a minor in advertising. She is originally from New Jersey, but now calls Miami home. Prior to Harris Media, she worked as a Digital Marketing Coordinator for Airoyal Company, where she generated sales leads, executed targeted email marketing campaigns, and managed digital content across LinkedIn and other marketing channels.

Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with friends and family, going on walks, traveling, trying new restaurants, and enjoying everything Miami has to offer.

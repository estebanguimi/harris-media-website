BIO

Johnny Benitez is a Senior Creative Producer / Videographer at Harris Media Group, where he combines creative storytelling with technical expertise to produce high-impact video and multimedia content. With over seven years of experience in digital production, Johnny has developed a diverse portfolio spanning branded campaigns, social media content, and long-form storytelling.

Before joining HMG, Johnny led creative efforts for agencies and organizations across South Florida. He has produced digital campaigns, managed media teams, and delivered fast-turnaround content that connects with audiences. His work reflects a passion for cinematic storytelling, sharp editing, and visual strategy that drives engagement and results.

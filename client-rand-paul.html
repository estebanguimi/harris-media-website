THE BRIEF

A NEW STANDARD FOR DIGITAL

Senator Rand Paul’s 2016 Presidential campaign was built on a movement of energized, online-first supporters. The challenge was to organize that energy into something a campaign could actually use.

Vincent Harris ran digital operations for the Kentucky Senator’s presidential run. The work set a new standard for digital strategy in politics, boosted his online impact and positioned him as a digitally savvy GOP candidate — enough that Politico profiled “Rand Paul’s internet army.”

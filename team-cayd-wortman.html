BIO

Cayd Wortman is an Account Executive at Harris Media LLC, where he supports statewide and local clients through full stack communications, grassroots execution, and digital strategy. Based in Waterville, Maine, Cayd brings a strong understanding of Maine voters and communities, pairing audience first messaging with creative content production that helps campaigns connect and perform.

A graduate of the New England School of Communications, Cayd holds a B.S. in Mass Communications, along with certificates in Web Media and Digital and Social Media Marketing. Prior to joining Harris Media in 2025, he served as Communications Manager for the University of Maine Alumni Association.

At Harris Media, Cayd’s work spans the full lifecycle of modern campaign communications, including designing campaign graphics and video content, scheduling and managing social media, writing and sending campaign emails, supporting fundraising efforts, and coordinating closely with campaign teams. He is also deeply involved in grassroots work, attending client events to capture authentic supporter footage and testimonials, and supporting ballot access efforts through on the ground signature gathering.Outside of work, Cayd embraces Maine’s outdoors and simple living. He enjoys time in the woods, fishing, golfing, hiking, and snowmobiling whenever he can.

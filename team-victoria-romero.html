BIO

Born and raised in Miami, Victoria is a Senior Account Executive at Harris Media LLC, where she manages digital communication strategies, content development, and social media engagement for clients. Although she never imagined working in politics, Victoria was drawn to HMG’s values, which align with her own. She looks forward to contributing to the company's continued success through her background in content creation and copywriting, with a focus on crafting impactful political messaging and advocacy campaigns.

Outside of her professional work, Victoria serves in her local church in Miami, FL, taking on individual roles in different departments: graphic design, sound, logistics and administrative. These experiences have allowed her to enhance her leadership skills, collaborate with multiple teams, and contribute to the creative direction of the church’s branding.

In her spare time, Victoria enjoys traveling and experimenting with new, delicious recipes, always seeking new experiences and culinary adventures.
